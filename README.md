The Dark Lord has ruled for three hundred years. He keeps the tax ledgers, takes the ravens' dawn reports, and guards the sword and the crown in the vault. The hero has come for the whole little ceremony — and the old villain, mid-sentence, keeps trailing off toward the fire. The tiredness is in the pauses and the deflections, never announced; the sting lands in the last line, when he catches himself and remembers his role.
